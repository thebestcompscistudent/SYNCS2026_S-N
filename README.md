# SYNCS2026_S&N
SYNCSHACK2026 Submission 

### Find your people. Discover your culture. Build your world.

Culture isn't one thing. It's made up of the people, places, food, traditions and experiences that connect us.

Block is a community and cultural discovery platform designed to help people reconnect with their culture and discover the communities around them.


## 🌍 The Problem

For many people, connection to culture doesn't come automatically.

Growing up away from our cultural communities, we often pick up pieces along the way:

- A dish our family makes
- A language we only partly speak
- A festival we attend once a year
- Stories passed down from our parents
- A restaurant we know about
- A community we don't know how to access

We collect these pieces, but don't always know how they fit together.

At the same time, information about cultural events and communities is scattered across Instagram, Facebook, event platforms, university societies, community websites and word-of-mouth.

The result: People who want to connect with their culture often don't know where to start.



## 💡 Our Solution

**Block brings the pieces together.**

Block is a single platform where users can:

- Discover cultural and social events
- Explore cultural places and events nearby
- Find cultural and halal food
- Discover and connect with communities
- Find people to attend events with
- Save events they're interested in
- Keep registered events in one calendar
- Participate in moderated community discussions
- Connect through verified accounts

Rather than telling people what their culture is, Block gives them the building blocks to explore it for themselves.



# Blocks That Make Up the World

Our project was inspired by the hackathon theme:

> **"Blocks that make up the world."**

We interpreted this through the idea that **a person's cultural world is made up of many individual blocks.**

### People
The communities and friendships that connect us.

### Events
The festivals, workshops, celebrations and gatherings we experience.

### Food
The dishes, restaurants and cuisines that carry culture.

### Places
The physical spaces where culture exists around us.

### Knowledge
The traditions, stories, languages and experiences we discover.

### Conversations
The people we meet and the questions we ask.

Individually, these are just blocks. Together, they build a world.



# Features

## Cultural & Social Events

Discover cultural and social events happening nearby.

Users can:

- Browse events
- View event details
- Save events
- Register for events
- Contact event organisers
- See upcoming registered events


## Saved Events

Found something interesting but not ready to commit?

Users can star events and save them for later.

Saved events become part of their personal collection of cultural experiences.



## My Calendar

Once a user registers for an event, it can be added to their calendar.

This creates a simple place to keep track of upcoming cultural experiences.



## 🗺️ Cultural Map

Explore the cultural world around you through an interactive map.

Users can discover:

- 🎉 Events
- 🍜 Restaurants
- 🏛️ Cultural organisations
- 👥 Community spaces
- 📍 Other culturally relevant places



## 🍜 Food Discovery

Food is one of the most accessible ways to connect with culture.

Block helps users discover:

- Halal restaurants
- Cultural restaurants
- Grocery stores
- Markets
- Cafes and bakeries

Users can explore food based on cuisine and dietary requirements.



## 👥 Community

Culture is not only about places and events — it's about people.

Users can participate in a moderated community space where they can:

- Ask questions
- Find people with similar interests
- Ask about cultural events
- Find someone to attend an event with
- Meet new people
- Share recommendations



## Go Together

Going to a new event alone can be intimidating.

Block allows users to express interest in attending an event with other members of the community.

Instead of:

> "I'd love to go, but I don't want to go alone."

Users can find:

> "Other people who want to go too."



## Safety & Verification

Because Block facilitates real-world connections, safety is important.

The platform is designed around:

- Verified accounts
- Community moderation
- Reporting and blocking
- Community guidelines

Our goal is to create a space where people can make genuine connections safely.



# Example User Journey

Meet Sarah.

Sarah grew up in Australia and has always felt somewhat disconnected from her cultural background.

She knows some traditions and foods from her family, but doesn't know where to find the wider community.

### 1. Discover

Sarah opens Block and discovers cultural events happening nearby.

### 2. Explore

She finds a cultural festival and saves it.

### 3. Find Food

Block shows culturally relevant restaurants near the event.

Sarah discovers a restaurant she's never tried.

### 4. Connect

Sarah sees other verified users interested in the festival.

She posts asking if anyone wants to attend together.

### 5. Attend

Sarah registers for the event.

It is added to her calendar.

### 6. Build

At the event, Sarah discovers another community and another cultural workshop.

She adds those experiences to her journey.

One block leads to another.

Her cultural world gets bigger.



# The Bigger Idea

Block isn't trying to define someone's culture.

Culture is personal.

For one person, it might be:

> Food + family + language

For another:

> Music + community + festivals

For someone else:

> Religion + traditions + people

Block gives users the freedom to decide which blocks matter to them.

### We don't build the world for them.

### We give them the blocks to build it themselves.



# Future Vision
